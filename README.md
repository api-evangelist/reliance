# Reliance, Inc.

Reliance, Inc. (NYSE: RS) is the largest metals service center operator in North America, distributing approximately 100,000 metal products to over 125,000 customers. The company operates through 75+ distinct brands across 300+ locations in the US, Canada, and internationally.

Reliance distributes aluminum, brass, bronze, carbon and alloy steel, copper, titanium, stainless steel, and specialty metals. The company provides value-added processing — cutting, shearing, sawing, leveling, blanking, stamping, and custom fabrication — alongside just-in-time delivery and inventory management services.
